# Demo Script (30–60 Seconds)

### Step 1: What I open
I open the Streamlit application in my web browser.
*(On screen: The clean StudyBuddy interface with the "Privacy First" notice visible).*

### Step 2: What I enter
I open `demo_notes.txt` (Computer Networks notes), copy the text, and paste it into the large text area.
*(On screen: The text area fills with dense study notes).*

### Step 3: What I click
I select the "Generate Quiz" radio button and click the primary "Generate" button.
*(On screen: Mouse clicks the options).*

### Step 4: What the AI does
The Streamlit app shows a loading spinner saying "AI is thinking locally...".
*(On screen: Loading indicator spins).*

### Step 5: What result I show
The app displays 5 quiz questions based exactly on the text, with answers hidden at the bottom. I scroll through the result.
*(On screen: Scroll down to reveal the generated quiz and answers).*

### Step 6: What I say about open AI
*(Spoken during the demo)*:
"My friend gets overwhelmed by long lecture notes but didn't want to upload private study materials to the cloud. So I built StudyBuddy. I just pasted some Computer Networks notes and asked for a quiz. Right now, this is running entirely locally on my machine using the Llama 3.2 3B open-weight model via Ollama. No data is sent to a third-party server, it's completely private, and there are zero API costs."
